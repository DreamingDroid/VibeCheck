## 🎬 3. Live Product Demo Script (Step-by-Step)

### Act 1: The Instant Content Shield (< 1 Minute)
1. **Show the Organizer Creation Form:**
   * Enter a test event with Telugu/Hindi slang (e.g. *"Weekend with dengu vibes"* or *"Crypto telegram leak meetup"*).
   * Click **Publish**.
   * **Highlight:** The submission is blocked in `< 5ms` with an explicit, localized safety reason.
2. **Show Clean Submission:**
   * Enter *"Vizag Sunset Acoustic Jam on RK Beach"*.
   * Upload an authentic event poster.
   * Click **Publish**.
   * **Highlight:** AI Vision verifies the poster, AI text scrutiny scores it 95/100, and it goes live in under 2 seconds.

---

### Act 2: Instagram Host Intelligence (< 1.5 Minutes)
1. **Show Organizer Verification Flow:**
   * Click **"Verify with Instagram"**.
   * Authenticate the Instagram Business account.
2. **Show the AI Intelligence Report:**
   * Reveal the structured report generated by Gemini 2.5 Flash:
     * *Past Offline Events Detected: 3*
     * *Primary Vibes: Acoustic Music, Indie Socials*
     * *Host Trust Score: 94 / 100*
     * *Monetization Risk: Low*
   * **Highlight:** *"No anonymous scammers. Only authentic creators with real community track records get organizer badges."*

---

### Act 3: Transparent Cancellation & Multi-Channel Alert (< 1 Minute)
1. **Book an RSVP as an attendee:**
   * Generate a pass with QR code.
2. **As the organizer, click "Cancel Event":**
   * Watch the real-time cascade:
     * Attendee receives an instant Telegram Bot notification.
     * Attendee receives an in-app notification.
     * Pass is marked as cancelled.
3. **Switch to Discovery Feed:**
   * Show that the event is still on today’s feed marked with a clear red `[🚨 CANCELLED]` badge so nobody travels to the venue in confusion.

---

### Act 4: 24/7 AI First-Responder Support (< 1 Minute)
1. **Open the "Help & Support" modal from the navigation header.**
2. **Type a natural question:** *"How do I get my entry QR code pass on my phone?"*
3. **Click "Submit Ticket":**
   * In 2 seconds, Gemini AI delivers the exact steps: checks the user's dashboard, provides Telegram bot shortcut `/passes`, and issues a unique ticket number `TC-xxxxxx`.
   * **Highlight:** *"Instant resolution at 2:00 AM on a Saturday night with zero support staffing costs."*
